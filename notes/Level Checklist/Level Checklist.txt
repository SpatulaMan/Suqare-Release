Test Of Speed (how fast can you throw shurikens into a dummy. Total number in it is your score. 1 point per shuriken in dummy. 20 second timer)
Test Of Evasion (shurikens being thrown at you. Dodge as many as possible. Gain 5 points per second, -1 per shuriken that hits you
